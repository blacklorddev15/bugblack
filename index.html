Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pair my WhatsApp number?
Open the dashboard from the Get Started button, select the bot you want to use, enter your WhatsApp number with the country code, and click Get Code. Then use the code in your WhatsApp Linked Devices settings.
Is the pairing permanent?
Yes. Once paired, your bot remains active while the hosting server is running. The dashboard is the central place to manage pairing, hosting, keys, and account activity.
How do I get a panel?
Simply tap any panel plan above, fill in the form, and we'll deploy your Pterodactyl panel instantly. Add SD to your wallet through Paystack, M-Pesa C2B Till, or M-Pesa STK Push, then manage your service from the dashboard.
Which payment methods are supported?
You can top up with Paystack hosted checkout, M-Pesa C2B Till, or M-Pesa STK Push. Your wallet balance is credited after the provider confirms the payment.
Can I connect my own app?
Yes. Registered users can generate a blacklord_ API key, configure signed webhooks, inspect delivery logs, and test read-only endpoints in the dashboard API Sandbox.
Do you offer a trial?
Yes. Use the 3-Hour Trial link in the menu to explore the platform before selecting a longer-term hosting plan.
