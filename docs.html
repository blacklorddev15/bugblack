Knowledge Base

Step-by-step guides on how to pair, host, and manage your WhatsApp bots.

Updated Today

Pairing Guide

1. Choose your bot type on the main page.
2. Enter your WhatsApp number in international format.
3. Tap "Get Pairing Code" and wait for the 8-digit code.
4. Open WhatsApp > Linked Devices > Link with Phone Number.

Hosting Guide

1. Top up your wallet using M-Pesa or Bank Transfer.
2. Select a Panel Plan from the dashboard.
3. Your credentials will be sent to your account dashboard.
4. Access the Pterodactyl panel to manage your files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use end-to-end encryption for all pairing sessions and never store your WhatsApp messages.

How do I top up my balance?

Go to the Wallet section on the main page and use the STK Push or Bank Transfer options.
